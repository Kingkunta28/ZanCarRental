    Driving in Zanzibar guide

    Essential guide

    Before you rent

    Seven things to know before you rent and drive in Zanzibar.

    1. 01

      Driving permit

      Every driver needs a Zanzibar driving permit ($25 per driver) on top of their home licence. We arrange it before you arrive.

    2. 02

      Transparent pricing

      Compare the full cost, not just the daily rate. Our car rental prices show every total with the permit included and no deposit.

    3. 03

      Right vehicle

      Check that the vehicle meets your travel needs. Consider terrain, passenger count and luggage space.

    4. 04

      Insurance coverage

      Check what your rental insurance covers. Our cars have third-party insurance, the basic cover required by law, which does not cover damage to the rental car itself. Some travel insurance policies offer cover for rental car damage.

    5. 05

      Road rules and safety

      Zanzibar drives on the left and police checkpoints are common.

    6. 06

      Pickup logistics

      Confirm clear pickup and drop-off locations and times to avoid any delays or confusion.

    7. 07

      Book ahead

      Reserve your vehicle in advance, especially during peak tourist season, to guarantee availability.

    Questions

    Can you deliver to my hotel?

    Yes. Add your hotel in the form and we will meet you there.

    How do I pay?

    Payment is arranged with us on WhatsApp after your booking is confirmed.

    Can I get a driver?

    Yes. Tell us in the notes and we will confirm the price.

    How old do I need to be to rent a car?

    The minimum age to rent a car is 21 years. Drivers under 25 may incur a young driver surcharge. Maximum age is 80 years.

    What documents do I need to rent a car?

    You need a valid driver's license, passport or ID card, and a credit card for the security deposit.

    Can I cancel my booking for free?

    Yes, you can cancel for free up to 48 hours before your pickup time. Cancellations within 48 hours may incur a fee.

    How do I make a booking?

    Choose a car, your dates and your pickup and return locations, then add your name and phone number. The summary shows your total, including any delivery fees. Tap "Send on WhatsApp" and send the message. We will confirm availability and payment with you on WhatsApp.

    Can I modify my booking after confirmation?

    Yes, you can modify your booking by contacting our support team at least 24 hours before pickup.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), bank transfers, and cash on delivery (for local bookings).

    Is my payment secure?

    Yes, all payments are processed through secure PesaPal payment gateway. Your card details are never stored on our servers.

    What does the insurance cover?

    Basic insurance includes CDW (Collision Damage Waiver) and Theft Protection. Full coverage adds zero excess and roadside assistance.

    Do you charge for delivery and collection?

    Delivery and collection are free at Zanzibar Airport and in Zanzibar Town (Stone Town). Anywhere else costs $25.00 for the pickup and $25.00 for the return, so picking up in Nungwi and returning in Paje costs $50.00 in total. The booking summary shows these fees before you send your request.
